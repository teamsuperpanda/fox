# Fox Trademark and Brand Policy

Fox is free software. The code can be forked, changed and sold under the MIT
licence. The Fox name and brand assets cannot: MIT grants copyright rights in
code, not trademark rights in a product name or logo.

This policy covers the Fox name and wordmark, the panda mark, the app icon, the
launch screen artwork, the banners, the screenshots and the store listing copy.
All of them are trademarks and brand assets of Team Super Panda Limited. They are
not covered by the MIT licence. See [ASSETS-LICENSE.md](ASSETS-LICENSE.md).

- The Fox application and its source code are copyright Team Super Panda

- The Fox name, logo, icons, screenshots and store listing copy are proprietary

- The Android application ID `com.teamsuperpanda.fox`, the iOS bundle ID and the

## What forks may do

You may fork the code and publish your own app, provided you:

1. **Rename it.** Choose a name that is not Fox and does not contain Fox in a way
   that suggests it is the official app.
2. **Replace every brand asset** in `assets/images/` with your own artwork. Do
   not reuse the panda mark, the icon or the launch screen.
3. **Change the application ID and bundle ID.** Do not publish under
   `com.teamsuperpanda.fox` or reuse the store listings.
4. **Credit the original** in a factual way, for example "Based on Fox by Team
   Super Panda" in your README or About screen. Nominative use like this is
   allowed.
5. **Make clear that your fork is not official.** Do not state or imply that Team
   Super Panda endorses, sponsors or maintains your build.

## What forks may not do

- Use the Fox name or wordmark as the name of a product, app, service, domain or
  social account
- Use the panda mark, app icon or other brand assets in your fork or its listing
- Publish under the Fox application ID, bundle ID or store listings
- Use the Fox name or assets in advertising in a way that suggests endorsement
- Register a trademark, domain or store listing that is confusingly similar to Fox

## Uses that are allowed

You do not need permission to:

- Refer to Fox by name in an article, review, tutorial, comparison or academic
  work, as long as the reference is accurate and not misleading
- Say that your project is "based on Fox", "a fork of Fox" or "compatible with Fox"
- Link to the Fox repository or store listings
- Redistribute unmodified official builds

## Reporting misuse

If you see a fork or a listing that uses the Fox name or assets in a misleading
way, report it through the repository issue tracker or
[www.teamsuperpanda.com](https://www.teamsuperpanda.com).

Team Super Panda Limited may update this policy as the project grows. The current
